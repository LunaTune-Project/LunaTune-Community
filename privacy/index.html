Privacy Policy

Draft — not yet operational

This draft describes the intended LunaTune Assistant community bot, not the music application's local storage. The bot is not yet operational.

For authorized administration and support, the bot may process Discord server, channel, role and user identifiers, command inputs and explicitly submitted reports as needed to perform requested actions. No advertising, profiling or sale of personal data is intended. Reports must be reviewed by users before submission; public GitHub issues and Discord posts are subject to those services' own policies and visibility.

Do not submit passwords, tokens, personal paths or listening history. Credentials for the bot must remain outside public source code. Access should be restricted to authorized maintainers.

Retention periods, hosting, operator contact details, legal bases and any applicable international transfers must be confirmed before operation. Users may contact maintainers to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, or complain to their relevant data protection authority.
